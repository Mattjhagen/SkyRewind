About SkyRewind

SkyRewind is a local aircraft tracking and sighting reporting system. It logs ADS-B position data from aircraft broadcasting their location, stores it for 30 days, and allows you to "rewind" historical traffic to match against sighting reports.

How It Works

  • Live Map: View real-time aircraft positions from ADS-B receivers
  • Report Sighting: Log observations with time, location, and description
  • Rewind & Match: Replay historical traffic to identify candidate aircraft

Important Note

When a sighting has "No ADS-B match found," it only means no cooperative aircraft broadcasting ADS-B were detected in the specified area and time window. Many aircraft do not broadcast ADS-B, and reception depends on line-of-sight and equipment limitations.
